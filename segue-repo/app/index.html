Segue

Every playlist has an order it wants to be played in.

Add your songs. Segue listens for tempo, rhythm and musical key, then arranges them so each track flows into the next, and plays the set with crossfades.

Drop audio files here

MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC or OGG. Folders are searched including subfolders. Everything stays on your computer.

What matters most

Shape of the set

How much of each song

Segue decides per song: it keeps the strongest sections, can skip a long intro, and leaves at a natural point such as the start of a breakdown. Energetic songs mid-set move on sooner; the opener and closer get more room.

Transitions

Next song comes in

Smart mode mixes out on a phrase boundary, locks the beats together, nudges the next song's tempo (its pitch shifts slightly, like a turntable) and swaps the basslines halfway. Songs without a steady beat get a fade. Loudness matching plays every song at the same level (quiet ones boosted by at most 6 dB) and a limiter catches the peaks where two songs overlap.

Keys use Camelot notation: neighbours on the wheel (8A → 9A) or the same number (8A → 8B) mix cleanly.
